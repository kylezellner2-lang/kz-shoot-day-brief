Anker · "Before the Moment"

1 IG feed Reel + TikTok repost45–60 sDraft due ~Nov 15Live by Nov 21

The calm few minutes before Kyle jumps, when everything gets charged and ready. The brand's theme: the moment that matters is the one just before you do the thing. The concept is approved. Shoot it as written plus the three brand adds.

Non-negotiables

  • Both products fully on screen, shown working, never explained with specs.
  • The exact line "Anker powers for my every moment." spoken or on screen, word for word.
  • The digital Moment Card shown in the video (image below).
  • Outdoors, in good natural light, no studio feel. Golden hour if possible.
  • At least two scene transitions.
  • Say "Anker" plus the product names: Anker Power Bank, Anker Charger.

Don'ts

  • No other charging brands in frame: no other power banks, chargers, or branded cables.
  • No spec reading (mAh, watts) and no opening on specs. Open on the feeling.
  • No visible logos from other sponsors (1win, DJI, C4 etc.). Tape over camera logos.
  • No commercial music. Natural sound plus a royalty-free track.
  • No blurred or pixelated footage. Nothing that reads as reckless.
  • The Power Bank stays on the ground. It doesn't go on the jump.

Bumble · I'm Dating on Bumble

1 IG Reel30–60 sDraft due Nov 15Live Nov 19, fixed

Super chill and low pressure. Kyle travels full-time and usually does this stuff alone. Recently he's been dating on Bumble and meeting people to do fun things with. This is a skydiving video first, with Bumble as part of the story. No big app moments, no text on screen telling people he's dating on Bumble.

The vibe

  • Story first, Bumble second. Bumble's own brief asks for this: "candid over polished", "never dating-coach energy."
  • Shot like his normal content: getting in the plane, jumping, hanging out. The phone and voice-over bits can be filmed anywhere with nice light.
  • The app is optional. If the videographer wants a quick shot of Kyle tapping into Bumble on his phone, fine. No text overlays about dating.
  • The date is simple: she shows up, they hug, they walk to the plane, they jump together. Nothing staged or cheesy.

Bumble's rules

  • Say "Bumble" within the first 10 seconds. Both scripts below do it in the voice-over, so no text on screen is needed.
  • Never say "match" (it reads as Tinder). Say "met" or "connect." Never say "swipe" or "swipe right."
  • Don't say "I'm partnering with Bumble" or "sponsored by." No pushy CTA like "Download Bumble."
  • No pink, red or purple clothing or styling. No visible logos on anyone's clothes. No shirtless shots.
  • If any Bumble profile is shown, blur the photos and hide identifying info. Everyone shown must be 18+.
  • Any text has to sit in the ad-safe zone, clear of the caption at the bottom and the icons on the right.
  • Music only from the Meta Commercial Sound Library. Note the song and artist when submitting.

Don'ts

  • No other dating apps anywhere, on screen or in notifications.
  • No third-party brands or products in frame (no Anker, no Carpe).
  • No alcohol or drugs anywhere near the Bumble story.
  • Kyle's profile must be verified. Bumble can ask for a reshoot if the brief isn't followed, so shoot extra coverage.
  • Caption (Kyle handles it): #BumblePartner and @bumble at the end.

Carpe · Flip Challenge Ad

1 master edit for Kyle's page~30 s · 9:16+ extra hooks, film onlyShot at the drop zone

Kyle does as many flips as he can out of the plane. The more flips, the bigger the viewer's discount on Carpe, up to 60% off. He has the stick in his hand during the flips and puts it on in freefall. The air footage comes from the camera flyer. You film everything on the ground and edit the master.

Carpe's must-haves

  • Kyle puts Carpe on in freefall, on camera (air footage).
  • He says the deal on the ground before the jump.
  • A flip counter on screen that ticks with every flip, then shows the final discount. Carpe navy and orange.
  • The final count said to camera after landing.
  • Dry-pit check after landing.
  • The CTA mentions the gift set and the money-back guarantee.
  • Plain dark tee, no logos.

What Kyle says, and doesn't

  • Say: it's an antiperspirant; the flip deal; the gift set (wash, wipes, tote) and the money-back guarantee.
  • Never: more than 60% off; "just deodorant"; any "99%" claim; medical claims; "Black Friday."

The discount: Kyle does as many flips as he can, and the % is set from that count, up to 60% max. The editor shows it on the counter.
